How the scanner works

Early Buy: a developing 15M candle shows bullish reversal behaviour near the lower Bollinger Band, with price structure and volume checks.

Early Sell / Short: the reverse behaviour near the upper Bollinger Band. On spot markets this is a bearish alert, not a spot short order.

Confirmed: the completed 1H candle must show directional follow-through, including a close beyond the preceding candle's high or low. 4H trend is displayed separately.

Wait for Retest: the move has already travelled too far relative to recent volatility, so the scanner avoids presenting it as a fresh actionable entry.

Early alerts can disappear before the candle closes. No technical indicator guarantees a profitable trade. Test with paper trading before risking real funds.
