How signals work

EARLY BUY: the live 15M candle turns green, price rises above the previous candle's close, and reversal/volume conditions support upward movement.

EARLY SHORT: the reverse bearish conditions. On Binance spot this is a bearish alert, not an actual short trade.

CONFIRMED: the last closed 1H candle agrees with the signal direction. 4H trend is shown separately so you can judge whether the setup aligns with the broader trend.

WAIT FOR RETEST: the move has already travelled too far relative to its recent volatility. Do not chase it.

Targets are based on risk multiples (1R, 2R, 3R). Support, resistance, fees, slippage and sudden news can make targets unrealistic. Test the method before risking money.
